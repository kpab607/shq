What is the trial drip?

When someone starts a ShipperHQ trial, they automatically enter a drip email sequence tailored to who they are. The system figures out whether they're brand new, an existing customer trying another platform, or a past customer who churned — and routes them to the right emails.

Net new — what makes a contact qualify

No paying accounts at any associated company
Not marked as a customer in HubSpot lifecycle stage
Has a business email domain (not gmail/yahoo)
Shopify/Shopify Plus accounts don't count as "paying" here — those contacts are treated as net new on other platforms

Customer — what makes a contact qualify

Any company they're linked to has an active, paying SHQ account
That account's trial ended more than 1 day ago
Account platform is not Shopify or Shopify Plus
Highest priority — one paying account overrides any number of churned or trial accounts at the same company

Churned — what makes a contact qualify

Has at least one SHQ account flagged as churned
That account is not a child/sub-account
Trial ended more than 1 day ago
Not currently paying on any other account (if they are, they're a Customer, not Churned)
From trial start to first email
1
Trial starts in the app

A new SHQ Account is created and synced to HubSpot. The contact enters the eligibility pool.

2
Triage workflow fires

Custom code checks the contact's company, then all SHQ Accounts linked to that company. It applies the Customer → Churned → Net New priority logic.

3
Contact gets stamped

The "Marketing Drip Stage" property is set to one value — Stage 1 – Welcome Email, Stage 1 – Customer, or Stage 1 – Churned. One contact, one value, one drip.

4
Drip workflow triggers

Each drip only fires when two things are true: the right stage value AND segment membership confirms the classification. Double-gated so nothing fires by accident.

Why does this matter? The old system used "NOT a member of" logic, which defaulted to true before HubSpot finished evaluating — causing customers and churned contacts to receive the net new welcome email by mistake. The new system only proceeds when membership is positively confirmed.

Trial contacts by drip eligibility — what each bucket means
Entered net-new drip
Contact was classified as net new and received the welcome sequence. This is the expected state for most trial contacts.
Not eligible for net-new drip
Contact was classified as Customer or Churned. They're being held until those drips are built — not lost, just waiting.
Didn't qualify
Has a company but failed other eligibility criteria — competitor status, internal domain, sandbox account, spam flag, etc. Expected and correct.
No company — not eligible
Gmail/Yahoo signup — HubSpot didn't auto-create a company. Known limitation until the app integration update ships. Filter this out for day-to-day monitoring.

What to watch for: If "Entered net-new drip" drops to zero, check that the triage workflow is on. If "Didn't qualify" spikes suddenly, a segment criterion may have changed unexpectedly.
